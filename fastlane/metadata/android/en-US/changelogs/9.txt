What's new in 1.5.0:

- Shake to Skip: shake your phone to skip tracks
- Flip to Pause: flip your phone face-down to pause
- Smart Playlists built from rules like Recently Added or decade
- Edit a local song's title, artist, album, or year from Now Playing
- Search LRCLIB directly to manually match lyrics
- Choose your app font: Space Grotesk, IBM Plex Sans, Outfit, Inter, Plus Jakarta Sans, or Silkscreen
- Choose a Now Playing backdrop style: Pure Black, Ambient Glow, or Liquid Gradient
- Fixed shuffle sometimes duplicating songs in the queue
- Fixed edited song tags not showing up until a restart
- Fixed Smart Playlists not letting you add songs
- Smoother scrolling through long lists
