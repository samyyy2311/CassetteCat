- Consistent back button navigation across the player sheet, drive mode, and start screens
- Better lyrics search: side-by-side artist and title search with cleaner match results
- Refined track actions sheet: categorized menu options, smoother scrolling, and clearer active indicators
- Direct in-app updates: download and install new releases from Settings without needing a browser
