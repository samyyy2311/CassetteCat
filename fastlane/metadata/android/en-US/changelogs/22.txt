What's new in 1.7.4:

- Smoother shuffle: toggling it no longer cuts off what's playing, and switching it off restores your original queue order
- Pull to refresh everywhere: songs, albums, artists, playlists, liked tracks, and folders
- Radio got a real upgrade: skip stations like normal tracks, filter by genre with quick pill buttons, and play/pause right from the station card
- Polished Now Playing controls, smoother lyrics scroll, and fixed jumping bottom sheets
- New vinyl record empty state, fallback artwork for missing covers, and desktop-style bordered buttons
- Fixed a crash on the Home screen while the library was still loading
