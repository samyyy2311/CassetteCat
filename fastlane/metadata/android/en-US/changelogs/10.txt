What's new in 1.6.0:

- Drive Mode: a large-touch-target playback view that launches automatically when you connect to your car's Bluetooth
- Wave to Skip: wave your hand over your phone to skip tracks
- Audio quality badge on Now Playing shows Lossless/Hi-Res, bit depth, and sample rate
- Write your own lyrics for a song and optionally contribute them to LRCLIB
- Smart Playlists support mutually-exclusive rule groups
- Fonts are now fully bundled offline, added VT323 and Monocraft, no more Google Play Services dependency for fonts
- Fixed playback sometimes stopping early instead of moving to the next queued song
- Fixed the seek bar snapping back after a manual seek
- Fixed Equalizer Reset re-enabling a disabled equalizer
- Fixed radio search showing results for the wrong country when switching filters quickly
- Fixed nearby Listening Room discovery running in the background after leaving the screen
- Reduced memory usage from playlist cover images
