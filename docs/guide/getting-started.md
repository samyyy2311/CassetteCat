# Getting started

CassetteCat plays music stored on your phone, music on your own Subsonic or Jellyfin server, and internet radio. It needs Android 8.0 or newer.

## Install

Pick one:

- **Google Play**: [CassetteCat on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=in.caffeinelabs.cassettecat). Updates arrive through the Play Store.
- **GitHub**: download `CassetteCat-vX.Y.Z.apk` from the [latest release](https://github.com/samyyy2311/CassetteCat/releases/latest) and open it. Android asks you to allow installs from your browser or file manager the first time. Each release also has a `.sha256` file if you want to check the download.
- **Obtainium**: with [Obtainium](https://github.com/ImranR98/Obtainium) installed, [add CassetteCat in one tap](https://apps.obtainium.imranr.dev/redirect?r=obtainium://add/https://github.com/samyyy2311/CassetteCat), or add `https://github.com/samyyy2311/CassetteCat` by hand. Obtainium then installs new GitHub releases as they come out.

The app can also tell you about new GitHub releases: **Settings > Check for Updates**.

## First launch

The setup takes about a minute and every step can be changed later in **Settings**.

1. **Welcome**: tap **Set up my library**.
2. **Permissions**: CassetteCat asks for:
   - **Music and audio**, to find the songs on your phone. Without it, only streaming and radio work.
   - **Notifications**, to show playback controls outside the app and on the lock screen.
   - **Nearby devices**, only used to pair a CassetteCat hardware player. You can skip it.
3. **Choose your music folders**:
   - **All music** finds audio anywhere Android allows. Start here if you're unsure.
   - **Only these folders** builds the library from folders you pick. Use it if your phone also holds podcasts, voice notes or audiobooks you don't want mixed in.
   - **Everything except these folders** scans everything but the folders you pick.
4. **Make it yours**: pick an accent colour, font, Now Playing backdrop, start screen and gestures. If you already have a CassetteCat backup file, tap **Restore from Backup** here to bring back your playlists, favourites and settings.
5. **Add your listening device**: this is for the CassetteCat hardware player. Tap **Skip** if you don't have one.

Your library appears on **Home** once the scan finishes. If it stays empty, see [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md#my-songs-dont-show-up).

## Finding your way around

- **Home**: quick picks, a shuffle button for your whole library, and sections such as Heavy Rotation, Recently Played, Recently Added and Forgotten Favorites.
- **Library**: Songs, Artists, Albums, Genres, Playlists and Folders. You can hide or reorder these tabs in **Settings > Customisation > Startup & Library > Library Tabs**.
- **Radio**: internet radio stations. See [Streaming, downloads and radio](streaming-and-radio.md#internet-radio).
- **Search**: searches song titles, artists and albums as you type.
- **Settings**: everything else.

The **mini-player** at the bottom shows what's playing. Tap it to open **Now Playing**. You can swipe across it to change tracks, and choose the extra button beside play and pause in **Settings > Customisation > Theme > Mini-Player Action**.
