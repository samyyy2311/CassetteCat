# Listening Room

A Listening Room lets people on the same Wi-Fi listen along with you. Everyone hears what the host plays, in the host's queue order. It works without an account or the internet, and nothing about the room is sent anywhere outside your network.

## Host a room

1. Open **Settings > Streaming & Services > Listening Room**, or **More > Listening Room** in Now Playing.
2. Tap **Start a nearby room**.

The screen shows a **Room code** and an address. Play music as usual; your guests follow along. Up to 8 guests can join. If nobody joins within two minutes, the room closes by itself.

To finish, tap **End nearby room**. Everyone is disconnected straight away.

## Join a room

1. Connect to the same Wi-Fi as the host.
2. Open **Listening Room** and look under **Nearby rooms**.
3. Tap the room, then **Join on this Wi-Fi**.

While you're in a room, your phone follows the host's playback and queue. The source is shown as **Room**. Tap **Leave room** to go back to your own music.

### If the room doesn't appear

Some networks stop devices from finding each other, for example office, hotel and guest Wi-Fi. Ask the host for the address and code on their screen, then use **Join by address**. The address looks like `192.168.1.30:41234#K7Q2PX`; enter all of it.

## Good to know

The host's phone streams the audio to guests, so songs stored only on the host's phone play for everyone.
