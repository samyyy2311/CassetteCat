# Library and playlists

## Choosing which music appears

**Settings > Library & Hardware > Scan Folders** sets where CassetteCat looks for music on your phone:

- **All music**: everything Android's media scanner has found.
- **Only these folders**: tap **Add folder** and pick each folder to include.
- **Everything except these folders**: pick folders to leave out, such as a voice recorder or podcast folder.

Two more filters are in **Settings > Customisation > Storage & Cache**:

- **Ignore Short Audio Clips** hides anything shorter than 30 seconds, such as ringtones and voice notes.
- **Duplicates** lists songs saved more than once, with the bitrate of each copy, so you can delete the copy you don't want. Deleting removes the file from your phone.

New files usually appear on their own. To rescan now, pull down on the Library screen.

## Browsing

The Library has six tabs: **Songs**, **Artists**, **Albums**, **Genres**, **Playlists** and **Folders**. In **Settings > Customisation > Startup & Library** you can:

- choose the tab that opens first (**Default Library Tab**) and hide or reorder tabs (**Library Tabs**);
- set **Grid Columns** for albums and artists (2, 3 or 4);
- choose **Song Row Density**: **Detailed** shows album and quality badges, **Compact** fits more songs;
- set the **Default Sort Order** (title, artist or album);
- turn **Show Audio Format Badges** on to see FLAC, MP3, AAC and HI-RES labels on each song.

Tap the refine button at the top of the Library to filter by source (phone, Subsonic, Jellyfin), show only favourites, downloads or recently added songs, and change the sort order. The A to Z rail on the right edge jumps through long lists.

## Song options

Tap the three dots beside a song for:

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Play Next** | Puts the song right after the current one. |
| **Add to Queue** | Adds it to the end of the queue. |
| **Add to Playlist** | Saves it to one of your playlists. |
| **Edit Details** | Changes the title, artist, album, genre or year shown in CassetteCat. |
| **Search Album Cover Online** | Finds artwork online and applies it. You can also pick an image from your phone. |
| **Reset to Original Cover** | Removes a cover you applied and goes back to the embedded one. |
| **Download** | Saves a streamed song for offline playback. |
| **Delete from Device** | Permanently deletes the file from your phone. |
| **Play Next on Computer** | Shown when your phone is paired with CassetteCat Desktop. See [Using CassetteCat with your computer](desktop.md). |

**Edit Details** doesn't change the audio file. Your changes are stored in CassetteCat and only the fields you edit are replaced, so other apps still see the original tags. **Revert to Original Tags** in the same sheet undoes them.

### Selecting several songs

Long-press a song to start selecting, then tap others or use **Select All**. The bar at the bottom lets you queue, add to a playlist, download, change the cover or edit tags for all of them. When editing several songs, only the fields you change are saved; fields that differ between songs show **Mixed**.

## Playlists

Open **Library > Playlists**.

- **New playlist**: name it, then add songs from the playlist with **Add Songs**.
- **Create Smart Playlist**: a playlist that fills itself from rules such as **Recently Added (Last 30 Days)**, **Favorited Tracks Only**, **Long Jams (> 5 min)**, **Quick Bites (< 3 min)**, or a decade from the 90s to the 2020s.
- **Import playlist**: opens an `.m3u` or `.m3u8` file and matches its songs against your library. A message tells you how many matched.

Ready-made smart playlists also appear here: **Top 50 Most Played**, **Recently Added**, **Forgotten Gems**, **Heavy Rotation**, **Extended Mixes** and **Vintage Classics**.

The options on a playlist include:

- **Change Cover**: a photo, an icon or an emoji.
- **Download All**: saves every streamed song in it for offline playback.
- **Export as M3U8**: saves the playlist as a file. Only songs stored on your phone are included; streamed songs are skipped and you're told how many.
- **Rename Playlist** and **Delete Playlist**.

You can also save whatever is in the queue as a playlist from Now Playing: **More > Save Queue as Playlist**.

## Favourites

Tap the heart on any song to like it. Liked songs are in **Liked Songs** in the Library and on Home. With **Settings > Customisation > Storage & Cache > Auto-Cache Favourite Tracks** on, liked songs from a server are also cached for offline use.

## Listening Record

**Settings > Library & Hardware > Listening Record** shows your most played artists, songs, albums and genres by month or year, your recent listens, busiest times and milestones. A song counts as played once you've heard 90% of it.

- **Save these tracks as a playlist** turns a month's most played songs into a playlist.
- **Share Rewind** makes a poster of your month or year to share.

Listening data stays on your phone. It only goes elsewhere if you turn on scrobbling, pair with CassetteCat Desktop, or have Android's own backup turned on for your phone. To stop recording it, turn off **Settings > Privacy & Security > Collect listening activity**. To clear it, use the clear button on the Listening Record screen.
