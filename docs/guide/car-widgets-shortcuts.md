# Car, widgets and shortcuts

## Drive Mode

Drive Mode is a full-screen player with large buttons, meant to be used at a glance.

- **Open it**: tap **Drive Mode** on Home, or **More > Drive Mode** in Now Playing.
- **Open it automatically**: turn on **Settings > Customisation > Audio Engine > Auto Drive Mode**. Drive Mode then opens whenever your phone connects to your car's Bluetooth audio.
- **Leave it**: tap **Exit Drive Mode**.

## Home screen widget

The widget shows the current song and its artwork with previous, play/pause and next buttons, styled as a cassette.

1. Long-press an empty spot on your home screen and choose **Widgets**.
2. Find **CassetteCat** and drag the widget onto your home screen.

## Quick Settings tile

The tile plays and pauses from the notification shade and shows what's playing.

1. Swipe down twice from the top of the screen to open Quick Settings.
2. Tap the edit (pencil) button.
3. Drag the **CassetteCat** tile into your active tiles.

## App shortcuts

Long-press the CassetteCat app icon for:

- **Shuffle all**: shuffles your whole library.
- **Play favorites**: plays your liked songs.
- **Radio favorites**: opens your favourite radio stations.

Drag a shortcut onto your home screen to keep it there.

## Lock screen and notification

While music plays, the notification and the lock screen show the song with play, pause and skip controls. Headphone and Bluetooth buttons work too. To pause automatically when headphones or a Bluetooth device disconnect, turn on **Settings > Customisation > Audio Engine > Pause on Disconnect**.
