# Using CassetteCat with your computer

[CassetteCat Desktop](https://github.com/samyyy2311/CassetteCat-Desktop) is the companion app for Windows, macOS and Linux. Once paired, your phone can:

- control the computer's playback as a remote;
- move what you're listening to, with the queue and position, from one to the other;
- keep likes in sync and copy playlists both ways;
- share listening history, so each app's Listening Record includes both;
- back up its data to the computer every day.

Everything travels over your own Wi-Fi. Nothing goes through the internet or an account.

## Pair your phone

You need the phone and the computer on the same Wi-Fi network.

**On the computer**

1. Open CassetteCat Desktop and go to **Settings > Network & Services**.
2. Under **Phone Remote**, turn on **Control From Your Phone**.

The same section shows a **Pairing code** and an address. You only need them if automatic pairing doesn't work.

**On the phone**

1. Open **Settings > Library & Hardware > Desktop Remote**. You can also tap the **Devices** button in Now Playing, beside the lyrics and queue buttons, then **Set up a computer**.
2. Under **Computers on this Wi-Fi**, tap your computer. If it isn't listed, tap **Search again**.
3. The phone shows **Click Allow on [computer] to connect**. On the computer, a box asks **Connect [phone]?**. Click **Allow**.

The phone now shows **Paired**. Only allow phones you own. A paired phone can control playback, sync your library data and back up to that computer.

### Pair by address instead

If your computer isn't found, often because of a guest network or a router that blocks devices from seeing each other:

1. On the computer, click **Copy Address** in Phone Remote. It looks like `192.168.1.20:47800#ABC234`: the computer's address, then `#`, then the pairing code.
2. On the phone, enter the whole address, including the part after `#`.

**New Code** on the computer makes a new pairing code. Phones paired with the old code have to pair again.

## Control the computer

In **Desktop Remote**, tap your computer. The row changes to **Controlling from this phone**. Now Playing on the phone shows and controls what the computer plays: play, pause, skip, seek, volume, shuffle, repeat and the queue. Covers come from the computer.

Tap the row again to stop controlling it. The computer keeps playing.

While a phone is in control, the computer shows a phone icon in its player bar saying **Controlled from [phone]**.

## Move playback between phone and computer

- **Phone to computer**: tap **Devices** in Now Playing and choose your computer. Your song, queue and position move to the computer and the phone stops. The computer also appears in Android's own output switcher, in the quick settings media controls.
- **Computer to phone**: on the computer, click the phone icon in the player bar. The phone picks up the same song at the same position.

When the phone plays music itself, the computer shows **Playing on [phone]** with play, pause and next buttons, plus **Play Here** to move the music to the computer.

Songs are matched by title and artist, so a song moves only if both libraries have it.

## Likes and playlists

- **Likes** sync on their own both ways, for songs both libraries have.
- **Send a playlist to the computer**: open the playlist's options and choose **Send to Computer**. A playlist with the same name on the computer is replaced. You're told how many songs matched.
- **Get a playlist from the computer**: in **Library > Playlists**, tap **Get a playlist from your computer** and choose one.
- **Play a song next on the computer**: in a song's options, **Play Next on Computer**.

## Back up to your computer

The phone backs itself up to the paired computer once a day when you open the app. The backup includes your playlists, favourites, listening statistics, folder filters and settings. Server passwords are left out; see [Backup, restore and privacy](backup-and-privacy.md).

**Settings > Backup & Restore** has two related buttons under **Computer**:

- **Back Up to Computer** makes a backup now.
- **Restore from Computer** replaces the phone's data with the latest backup on the computer. Use it after resetting or replacing your phone.

The computer keeps the previous backup alongside the latest one.

## Stop using it

- **On the phone**: **Desktop Remote > Forget this computer**.
- **On the computer**: turn off **Control From Your Phone**, or click **New Code** so existing phones can no longer connect.

If something doesn't connect, see [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md#cassettecat-desktop).
