# CassetteCat documentation

## Using the app

Start with **Getting started** if you're new.

- [Getting started](guide/getting-started.md): installing, first launch, and finding your way around.
- [Library and playlists](guide/library-and-playlists.md): music folders, browsing, editing song details, playlists, smart playlists and your Listening Record.
- [Playback and sound](guide/playback-and-sound.md): Now Playing, gestures, audio settings, the equalizer, speed, the sleep timer and lyrics.
- [Streaming, downloads and radio](guide/streaming-and-radio.md): Subsonic, Navidrome and Jellyfin, offline downloads, internet radio, online services and scrobbling.
- [Using CassetteCat with your computer](guide/desktop.md): pairing with CassetteCat Desktop, remote control, moving playback, sync and backups.
- [Listening Room](guide/listening-room.md): listening together on the same Wi-Fi.
- [Car, widgets and shortcuts](guide/car-widgets-shortcuts.md): Drive Mode, the home screen widget, the Quick Settings tile and app shortcuts.
- [Backup, restore and privacy](guide/backup-and-privacy.md): backups, moving to a new phone, and what stays on your phone.
- [Troubleshooting](guide/troubleshooting.md): fixes for common problems, and how to report a bug.
