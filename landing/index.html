About

CassetteCat is an open-source music player project. It starts as an app, and is growing into a standalone player built around dedicated hardware.

Local Library
Scans your device with folder filtering and full metadata support.
Self-Hosted Streaming
Subsonic and Jellyfin, with offline caching.
EQ & AutoEq
A graphic equalizer plus calibration profiles for your headphones.
Internet Radio
Tens of thousands of live stations, searchable by name, tag, or country.
Synced Lyrics
Real-time lyrics from LRCLIB or embedded tags.
Encrypted Credentials
Hardware-backed AES-256. No accounts, no ads.
Listening Room
Beta
Sync playback with nearby devices over your local Wi-Fi.
Android Auto
Browse your library and control playback from the car.
Home Screen Widget
Play, pause, and skip from your home screen.
Scrobbling
Track plays with ListenBrainz and Libre.fm.
Backup & Restore
Export and restore your library, playlists, and settings.
Listening Stats & Rewind
Monthly listening time, top artists, and a shareable poster.

Credits

Built on open work.

CassetteCat exists because of these services, libraries, and design projects. The same list ships inside the app's Settings > Credits & Attribution.

Services & Data
  • LRCLIB, synced lyrics
  • Deezer, artist images
  • TheAudioDB, artist images & bios
  • Radio Browser, internet radio directory
  • GitHub, release update checks
  • Wikipedia & Wikimedia, bios (CC BY-SA 4.0)
  • MusicBrainz, metadata
  • Cover Art Archive, cover scans
  • ListenBrainz, open scrobbling
  • Libre.fm, GNU FM scrobbling
Core Audio & Architecture
  • AutoEq, headphone EQ curves
  • AndroidX Media3, playback engine
  • Android AudioFX, EQ & DSP
  • KotlinX Coroutines, async state
  • Jetpack DataStore, local storage
  • OkHttp, HTTP client
Design & Typography
  • Lucide Icons, iconography
