CassetteCat is a free and open-source Android music player built on a strict local-first, zero-telemetry architecture. We do not collect personal data, include no analytics or advertisements, and store your media library credentials exclusively on your physical device.

01.Scope and Summary

This policy applies to the CassetteCat application (in.caffeinelabs.cassettecat) published on Google Play, GitHub Releases, and F-Droid, as well as the website at cassettecat.caffeinelabs.in.

The application is designed for local media playback and direct self-hosted streaming. There is no central account creation, no sign-up wall, and no telemetry backend operated by CaffeineLabs.

02.Data We Collect

We collect zero personally identifiable information (PII).

We do not collect names, email addresses, device identifiers (IMEI / Android ID), location data, contact lists, or audio recordings. Playback history, playlists, and listening statistics are stored entirely in your device's local database and are never transmitted to CaffeineLabs.

03.Storage & Audio Access

CassetteCat requests standard audio read permissions on Android:

READ_MEDIA_AUDIO / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE
Used solely to scan, index metadata, and play audio files stored on your device's internal storage or SD card. Parsing occurs 100% locally.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS / FOREGROUND_SERVICE
Used to display the system media notification and lock screen playback controls while audio plays in the background.

Your audio files and metadata tags are never uploaded or synced to external servers by CassetteCat.

04.Server Credentials & Encryption

If you connect the app to a self-hosted media server (Subsonic, Navidrome, Gonic, Airsonic, or Jellyfin):

  • Your server endpoint URL, username, and authentication token/password are saved locally.
  • All credentials are encrypted using hardware-backed encryption via the Android KeyStore with AES-256-GCM.
  • Credentials are transmitted only to your designated self-hosted server endpoint over HTTPS to fetch your music stream.

05.Third Party Services

CassetteCat connects to the following external services to provide metadata, artwork, and lyrics. Each is individually toggleable in Settings > External Services, and all are enabled by default:

LRCLIB (lrclib.net)
Fetches synchronized scrolling lyrics using song title and artist name, for tracks with no lyrics embedded.
Deezer
Looks up artist images and album artwork using artist and album names.
TheAudioDB
Fallback source for artist images when Deezer has none available.
MusicBrainz & Cover Art Archive
Looks up open music metadata and archival CD/vinyl cover artwork.
Wikipedia & Wikimedia
Fetches "About" descriptions for artists and albums using their names.
GitHub
Checks github.com/samyyy2311/CassetteCat for newer app releases. Toggled separately in Settings > Updates, not in External Services.
ListenBrainz & Libre.fm (Optional)
Off by default. Only activates if you enter your own user token in Settings, after which playback logs are submitted to your chosen scrobbling profile.

You can enable Offline Blackout Mode in Settings at any time to immediately disable all outbound network requests across the app, overriding every toggle above.

06.Tracking, Analytics & Advertising

CassetteCat is strictly anti-tracking:

  • No Advertisements: No ad networks, banners, or rewarded video SDKs.
  • No Analytics SDKs: No Google Analytics, Firebase, Crashlytics, or Mixpanel.
  • No Trackers: No background fingerprinting, advertising IDs, or behavioral analytics.

07.Data Retention & Deletion

Because all data lives on your device, you retain absolute ownership. You can permanently delete all app data at any time by:

  • Tapping "Clear Credentials" or "Reset Listening Record" inside CassetteCat Settings.
  • Clearing data in Android System Settings (Settings > Apps > CassetteCat > Storage > Clear Storage).
  • Uninstalling the app from your device.

08.Your Rights Under the DPDP Act, 2023

CaffeineLabs is based in India, and this policy is designed to align with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act). Under the Act, a Data Fiduciary must honor a Data Principal's rights to access, correct, and erase their personal data, and must provide a way to raise grievances.

Because CassetteCat does not collect or store personal data on any server (see Section 2), these rights are already met by design: your data lives entirely on your device, where you can view, correct, or delete it directly, or by uninstalling the app (see Section 7).

For grievances or data-related requests, contact us via GitHub Issues (Section 12). If you choose to connect an optional third-party service (Section 5) using your own account or token, that service's own obligations under the DPDP Act and its own privacy policy apply to that data, not to CaffeineLabs.

09.Your Rights Under the GDPR (EEA & UK)

CassetteCat is distributed globally, including to users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and United Kingdom, where the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies. The GDPR gives you rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict, or port your personal data, and to object to its processing.

Because CassetteCat processes no personal data on any server, CaffeineLabs does not act as a data controller for information generated while using the app; it exists only on your device, under your direct control, and can be exported, corrected, or deleted at any time (see Section 7).

Where an optional third-party service in Section 5 processes data on your behalf (for example, a lyric search sent to LRCLIB, or a self-hosted server in Section 4), that service acts as an independent data controller under its own GDPR-compliant privacy policy, not CaffeineLabs.

10.Children's Privacy

CassetteCat is not directed at children under the age of 13. As stated in Section 2, the app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, regardless of age.
